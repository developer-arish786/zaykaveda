About us

The story of Zaykaveda

Zayka means taste; veda means knowledge. Zaykaveda is our promise that good food comes from knowing your ingredients, your spices and your guests.

Founded by Arish Shah, the restaurant serves two much-loved menus under one roof: North and South Indian dishes cooked in the tandoor and on the tawa, and the Indo-Chinese dishes — manchurian, hakka noodles, momos, chilli paneer — that India has made its own.

We cook to order, keep the menu honest and the prices fair, and confirm every online order personally.
